# Deviations and design changes

Every change to the study plan is listed here with its date, reason and effect, including changes made
**before** registration, so reviewers can see the full history. Changes after registration will also be
reported in the final paper.

| # | Date | Change | Reason | Effect | Before registration? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | 2026-10-26 | H2 equivalence bound changed from \|β\| < .05 (standardised) to ±5 percentage points of AI gain per 1 SD of logic; solvers tripled with 3 reasoning modes (84 solvers) | \|β\| < .05 needed about 3,400 solvers | H2 feasible with 87% power at a true zero | Yes |
| D2 | 2026-11-05 | Separate development seed (7110526) for pilot and synthetic runs; registered items locked until the freeze | Avoid any look at the registered items before registration | Registered items unseen by any model before the freeze | Yes |
| D3 | 2026-11-05 | Collapse gate G2 added: exclude a solver with > 90% the same answer in a choice family | A collapsed solver is not reasoning and distorts slopes | Pre-declared exclusion rule | Yes |
| D4 | 2026-11-05 | **Primary outcome for H1, H2 and H4 changed from the adjusted (ANCOVA) score to the gain score** (author's decision 14) | Pipeline validation: the ANCOVA gave 62 to 99% false alarms, because the solo score is a noisy measure of an ability the logic score measures more cleanly | False-alarm rate about 5 to 6%; ANCOVA kept as a labelled sensitivity analysis (S1, S2) | Yes |
| D5 | 2026-11-05 | Catch items (24) and gates G5 to G7 added | Known-answer checks before any measure is trusted | G6 can mark H4 UNINFORMATIVE; G5 and G7 add caution labels | Yes |
