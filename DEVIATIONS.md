# Deviations and design changes

Every change to the study plan is listed here with its date, reason and effect, including changes made
**before** registration, so reviewers can see the full history. Changes after registration will also be
reported in the final paper.

| # | Date | Change | Reason | Effect | Before registration? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | 2026-10-26 | H2 equivalence bound changed from \|β\| < .05 (standardised) to ±5 percentage points of AI gain per 1 SD of logic; solvers tripled with 3 reasoning modes (84 solvers) | \|β\| < .05 needed about 3,400 solvers | H2 feasible with 87% power at a true zero | Yes |
| D2 | 2026-11-05 | Separate development seed (7110526) for pilot and synthetic runs; registered items locked until the freeze | Avoid any look at the registered items before registration | Registered items unseen by any model before the freeze | Yes |
| D3 | 2026-11-05 | Collapse gate G2 added: exclude a solver with > 90% the same answer in a choice family | A collapsed solver is not reasoning and distorts slopes | Pre-declared exclusion rule | Yes |
| D4 | 2026-11-05 | **Primary outcome for H1, H2 and H4 changed from the adjusted (ANCOVA) score to the gain score** (author's decision 14) | Pipeline validation: the ANCOVA gave 62 to 99% false alarms, because the solo score is a noisy measure of an ability the logic score measures more cleanly | False-alarm rate about 5 to 6%; ANCOVA kept as a labelled sensitivity analysis (S1, S2) | Yes |
| D5 | 2026-11-05 | Catch items (24) and gates G5 to G7 added | Known-answer checks before any measure is trusted | G6 can mark H4 UNINFORMATIVE; G5 and G7 add caution labels | Yes |
| D6 | 2026-11-25 | **Harder hard-deduction items: a 4-level difficulty ladder** (author's decisions 15 and 19). Level 1 = the v0.2.0 puzzle, unchanged; levels 2 to 4 have 12, 16 and 20 statements, longer chains, and from level 3 "either/or" steps that need both cases checked. Items split evenly over levels; planted errors exactly half of each level × answer block | Pilot (2026-11-07): strong models scored 90% or more on hard deduction, leaving no room to show who gains | Ladder keeps weak models above the floor and strong models below the ceiling; level recorded per item and reported by level | Yes |
| D7 | 2026-11-25 | **Lenient number reading** (author's decision 16): the answer is the last number on the FINAL line ("FINAL: Tanya earned $365" = 365) | Pilot: the strict reader marked such replies unreadable and excluded a whole model on GSM8K (24% vs 44% when read leniently) | Fewer false exclusions by gate G3; 7 new known-answer cases in gate G1 | Yes |
| D8 | 2026-11-25 | **Always-thinking models kept, labelled `thinking`** (author's decision 17): their 3 modes are pooled into one solver unit; `thinking` added as a control when both kinds are present | These models reason at length whatever the mode prompt says, so their 3 "modes" are not 3 different solvers | 28 models = 26 × 3 modes + 2 pooled thinking models = 80 solver units (was 84) | Yes |
| D9 | 2026-11-25 | **Free APIs only, spread over days** (author's decision 18): runs pause when a key's free daily limit is used and continue from the saved answers; up to 4 keys per provider rotate; `plan` estimates the days | No budget for paid or rented hardware | USD 0; longer calendar time (see `python -m logicxai plan`) | Yes |
| D10 | 2026-11-25 | **Model list renewed** (author's decision 20): models up to about 14B run free on the author's own computer with Ollama; larger ones on free API tiers (Groq, Google AI Studio, Mistral) | Groq retired the Llama models; free APIs rarely offer small models | 22 local + 6 free-API solvers; sizes 0.36B to 27B (was 0.36B to 33B) | Yes |
| D11 | 2026-11-25 | **Assistant AIs changed** (author's decision 21): gpt-oss-120b (Groq) primary, Gemma 4 31B (Google) replication | Llama 3.3 70B retired on Groq; Qwen 2.5 72B not reachable free | Both free and open-weights; hidden reasoning is stripped before a solver sees a reply | Yes |
| D12 | 2026-11-25 | Pilot fixes: leak scan reads only the text the study adds (not the item); H4 reported "Not testable" with fewer than 3 models; statsmodels pinned; benchmark revisions pinned (LogiQA parquet branch); `check` no longer hangs | Problems found in the pilot (report 2026-11-21) | No change to hypotheses or tests | Yes |
